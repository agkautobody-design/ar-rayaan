# Hadi: questions the app cannot yet answer from verified content

Source: `docs/hadi_coverage.md`, rows marked MISS. Each topic below needs
content the OWNER or a scholar approves, with exact sources, before Hadi may
answer it. Nothing here was written into the app. Until then Hadi refuses
plainly and points to the closest screen and Search.

## Qur'an facts
- Longest and shortest surah, first and last surah revealed, what a juz or
  hizb is, what tafsir is, what Surah Yasin is about.
  (Counts of ayat per surah could be computed from the bundled Qur'an text;
  the claim about revelation order needs a named source.)

## Core terms
- Iman (and the pillars of iman), taqwa, riya, what hadith qudsi is, what a
  hadith is, imam Muslim, the four rightly guided caliphs.
- Meanings of inshallah, mashallah, astaghfirullah, bismillah.
- Al-Rahman, Al-Wadud (names of Allah as questions).

## Worship detail
- What to say in ruku, what invalidates prayer, the duha prayer,
  tahiyyat al-masjid, touching the Qur'an without wudu, fidyah detail,
  washing and burying the dead (the Huda janazah guides cover the steps; the
  questions as asked do not match them).
- Hajj detail: jamarat, miqat, talbiyah, the black stone, day of tarwiyah.
- Qurbani/udhiyah and the sunnah of slaughter; virtues of Dhul Hijjah.

## Seerah events as questions
- Badr, Uhud, the Trench, Isra and Miraj, the Cave of Hira, the Prophet's
  childhood and death. (The Stories seerah pack has chapters on these; title
  matching misses phrasing such as "what happened at".) A scholar-reviewed
  alias list for event questions would fix most of these with no new content.

## Calendar
- Muharram, Rajab, Shaban, Mawlid, the night of Baraat, the Hijri calendar.

## Family and character
- Walimah, treating a wife, birr al-walidayn, silat al-rahim, family ties,
  respecting elders, helping children love Islam, a husband-wife dispute,
  best character, modesty.
- New-Muslim questions: what to say to become Muslim, changing one's name,
  telling family.

## Wording and rulings (scholar must decide)
- Gelatin, cryptocurrency, tattoos. These are rulings; Hadi sends them to a
  scholar by design.

## Seed answers for the hosted cache
- If the owner wants a shipped seed cache, each seed answer is new religious
  text and needs approval with sources before it ships.
