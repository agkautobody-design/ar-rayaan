# Community rules and code of conduct (DRAFT)

**DRAFT. Not reviewed.** This text needs review by the owner and by a scholar
before any real member sees it. The in-app copy lives in
`lib/features/community/domain/community_rules_text.dart` and shows a DRAFT
banner until `kCommunityRulesAreDraft` is set to false. Version:
`2026-10-draft-1` (must equal `backend/src/versions.ts`). When the words
change, bump the version in both places so everyone accepts again.

No Qur'an, hadith or ruling is quoted here on purpose. Any such wording must
come from the scholar reviewer, not from the drafting.

## Who this is for
- Forums and meet-ups are for adults (18+). Children and teenagers never see them.
- Your display name is shown. Do not use your full name, phone or e-mail.

## Community opinion, not a ruling
- Everything members write is personal opinion, labelled "Community opinion".
- Do not give rulings (fatwa). Use Ask a scholar: members ask, only verified
  scholars answer, and their answers are labelled separately.
- Hadi never reads or learns from community posts.

## Speak with adab
- Kind words. No insults, mockery, backbiting.
- No takfir. No fighting between schools or groups. Sensitive topics have
  their own category; every post is read by a moderator first.

## Keep people safe
- No photos. No home addresses, phone numbers or e-mails (yours or others').
- No private messages in this phase. No asking to move to another app.
- Links only to sites a moderator has allowed. No advertising or money requests.

## Meet-ups
- Public venues only: mosque, community centre, library, halal cafe, park,
  school hall. Never a home.
- The organiser chooses who it is for: everyone, brothers, sisters, families.
- A verified organiser is checked by a person; others are read by a moderator
  first.
- Children only at "families" meet-ups run by a verified organiser, signed up
  by their guardian. A child's name is never shown.

## Report, hide, block
- Available on every post, thread and meet-up. The reporter is never named.
- A child-safety report hides the item at once for a person to review.

## Consequences
- Hide, warn, mute, suspend. You are told why. You can appeal; a different
  moderator decides. You can export or delete your data.
